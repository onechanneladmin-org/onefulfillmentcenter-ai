How this was researched
  • Search volume, 3-month and year-over-year change, Google Ads competition and top-of-page bids come from Google Keyword Planner (United States, English, Nov 2025 – Oct 2026), exported on 27 Nov 2026.
  • Keyword Planner shows ranges such as 100–1K because the Ads account has no recent spend. The ranges are shown as given.
  • "Ads competition" is how many advertisers bid. It is not how hard it is to rank organically.
  • Ranking feasibility is an estimate from who ranks on Google today (big brands, software vendors, list articles, smaller 3PLs). Connect Ahrefs to replace it with keyword difficulty and rank tracking.
  • City and industry keyword volumes were not collected, so location and industry pages are marked "validate demand first".
